Description
General & Descriptive:
GENERAL: The text does not relate to the writer’s personal experiences. It refers instead to general truths and to examples taken or adapted from other sources that connect to the theme or topic of the reflection but do not come from the writer’s own lived experience.


DESCRIPTIVE: MOREOVER, It focuses only on describing, depicting, reporting, and illustrating selected aspects of external, general examples and observations. It may go as far as showing how the author identifies the relevance of these general examples to the theme or topic of the reflection.
Specific & Descriptive
SPECIFIC: The text relates to the author’s personal examples and experiences. Personal pronouns (I, me, my, we, our) and the author’s specific examples take a primary position within the text. These examples are often individualistic and specific to the author’s lived experiences and points of view.

General & Reflective
GENERAL: The text does not relate to the writer’s personal experiences. It refers instead to general truths and to examples taken or adapted from other sources that connect to the theme or topic of the reflection but do not come from the writer’s own lived experience.


REFLECTIVE: HOWEVER, It goes beyond illustrating or describing; beyond the mechanical explanation of an example and moves into a deeper personal interpretation of these examples, where critical thinking and self-analysis (reflective practice) come into play. The examples are personally (by the author) evaluated in terms of their effectiveness and/or helpfulness in the author’s self-development.
