# Decarbonization Strategies and Pathways

## Overview

This document outlines practical strategies for reducing greenhouse gas emissions across different scopes and sectors, providing actionable guidance for organizations pursuing net-zero goals.

## Scope 1 Reduction Strategies

### 1. Energy Efficiency

**Building Operations:**
- HVAC optimization and smart controls
- Building envelope improvements (insulation)
- LED lighting with occupancy sensors
- Regular maintenance of equipment

**Industrial Processes:**
- Process optimization
- Heat recovery systems
- Variable speed drives on motors
- Equipment upgrades to high-efficiency models

**Expected Reduction:** 10-30% of Scope 1 emissions

### 2. Fuel Switching

**From Fossil to Low-Carbon:**
- Natural gas to biogas/biomethane
- Oil heating to electric heat pumps
- Coal to renewable thermal options
- Diesel generators to battery storage

**Electrification:**
- Electric boilers for process heat
- Heat pumps for heating/cooling
- Induction heating for industrial processes

**Expected Reduction:** 50-90% depending on grid carbon intensity

### 3. Fleet Electrification

**Vehicle Transition:**
- Battery electric vehicles (BEVs) for light-duty
- Hybrid vehicles as intermediate step
- Electric trucks for short/medium routes
- Hydrogen fuel cells for heavy-duty applications

**Infrastructure:**
- Workplace charging stations
- Route optimization for range management
- Charging schedule optimization

**Expected Reduction:** 70-100% (depending on electricity source)

### 4. Refrigerant Management

**Strategies:**
- Transition to low-GWP refrigerants
- Improved leak detection and maintenance
- Equipment upgrades
- Natural refrigerants (CO2, ammonia, hydrocarbons)

**Expected Reduction:** 50-99% of fugitive emissions

## Scope 2 Reduction Strategies

### 1. Energy Efficiency

**Electricity Reduction:**
- Equipment efficiency upgrades
- Lighting optimization
- Power factor correction
- Building automation systems
- Employee behavior programs

**Expected Reduction:** 10-40% of electricity consumption

### 2. Renewable Electricity Procurement

**Options (in order of impact):**

**On-site Generation:**
- Rooftop solar PV
- Building-integrated renewables
- On-site wind (where appropriate)

**Off-site Procurement:**
- Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)
- Virtual PPAs (financial agreements)
- Direct contracts with renewable generators

**Energy Attribute Certificates:**
- Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)
- Guarantees of Origin (GOs)
- International RECs (I-RECs)

**Considerations:**
- Additionality (new vs existing projects)
- Temporal matching (24/7 carbon-free)
- Geographic matching (same grid)

### 3. Demand Response

**Strategies:**
- Shift consumption to low-carbon periods
- Peak demand management
- Battery storage for load shifting
- Participation in grid programs

## Scope 3 Reduction Strategies

### Category 1: Purchased Goods and Services

**Strategies:**
- Supplier engagement programs
- Low-carbon procurement criteria
- Material efficiency and redesign
- Circular economy approaches
- Local sourcing

**Implementation:**
- Supplier carbon disclosure requirements
- Preferential terms for low-carbon suppliers
- Collaborative reduction programs
- Product carbon footprint specifications

### Category 4: Transportation and Distribution

**Strategies:**
- Modal shift (air → sea/rail)
- Route optimization
- Supplier consolidation
- Local sourcing
- Electrification of logistics partners

**Expected Reduction:** 20-60%

### Category 5: Waste

**Strategies:**
- Waste reduction at source
- Recycling program enhancement
- Composting organic waste
- Waste-to-energy partnerships
- Supplier packaging reduction

### Category 6: Business Travel

**Strategies:**
- Virtual meeting policies
- Train vs plane policies
- Travel approval systems
- Sustainable airline choices
- Carbon offsetting (as last resort)

**Policy Examples:**
- No flights for trips <4 hours by train
- Video conferencing as default
- Annual travel budgets per employee

### Category 7: Employee Commuting

**Strategies:**
- Remote work policies
- Public transport subsidies
- Cycling infrastructure and incentives
- Electric vehicle charging at workplace
- Carpooling programs

### Category 11: Use of Sold Products

**For Product Companies:**
- Energy-efficient product design
- Renewable energy integration
- Product-as-a-service models
- Customer behavior guidance
- End-of-life programs

## Sector-Specific Pathways

### Commercial Real Estate

**Key Levers:**
1. Energy efficiency retrofits (30% reduction)
2. Electrification of heating (40-60% reduction)
3. On-site renewables (10-30% reduction)
4. Green building certifications (LEED, BREEAM)
5. Tenant engagement programs

### Manufacturing

**Key Levers:**
1. Process optimization and efficiency
2. Electrification of process heat
3. Fuel switching to hydrogen/biofuels
4. Circular material flows
5. Supply chain engagement

### Transportation and Logistics

**Key Levers:**
1. Fleet electrification
2. Sustainable aviation fuel (SAF)
3. Route and load optimization
4. Modal shift
5. Last-mile innovation

### Financial Services

**Key Levers:**
1. Operational emissions (buildings, travel)
2. Financed emissions (portfolio decarbonization)
3. Green finance products
4. Client engagement
5. Climate risk management

## Implementation Framework

### Phase 1: Foundation (Year 1)
- Complete GHG inventory
- Set science-based targets
- Identify quick wins
- Build internal capability

### Phase 2: Efficiency (Years 1-3)
- Implement energy efficiency measures
- Reduce waste and water use
- Optimize operations
- Track and report progress

### Phase 3: Transition (Years 3-7)
- Shift to renewable energy
- Electrify operations
- Engage supply chain
- Update products/services

### Phase 4: Transformation (Years 7-15)
- Achieve deep decarbonization
- Address hard-to-abate emissions
- Scale carbon removal
- Reach net-zero operations

## Carbon Removal and Offsetting

### When to Use
- After maximizing internal reductions
- For genuinely unavoidable emissions
- As bridge solution while transitioning
- For residual emissions at net-zero

### Quality Criteria
- Additionality
- Permanence
- Verification
- No double counting
- Co-benefits

### Removal Options
- Nature-based solutions (forestry, soil carbon)
- Technical solutions (DAC, BECCS)
- Hybrid approaches

## Financial Considerations

### Investment Prioritization

**High ROI Opportunities:**
- LED lighting (2-3 year payback)
- Building automation (3-5 year payback)
- Solar PV (5-10 year payback)
- Heat recovery (2-4 year payback)

**Longer-Term Investments:**
- Fleet electrification
- Building retrofits
- Process electrification
- Green hydrogen

### Financing Options
- Internal capital
- Green bonds
- Sustainability-linked loans
- Energy service companies (ESCOs)
- Government incentives and grants
