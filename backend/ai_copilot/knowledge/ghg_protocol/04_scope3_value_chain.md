# Scope 3 Emissions: Value Chain Emissions

## Definition

Scope 3 emissions are all indirect emissions (not included in Scope 2) that occur in the value chain of the reporting company, including both upstream and downstream emissions.

Scope 3 emissions often represent the largest share of an organization's carbon footprint, frequently 70-90% of total emissions.

## The 15 Scope 3 Categories

### Upstream Categories (1-8)

#### Category 1: Purchased Goods and Services
All upstream emissions from production of products and services purchased by the organization.

**Calculation Methods:**
- **Supplier-specific**: Data from suppliers (preferred)
- **Hybrid**: Combine supplier and secondary data
- **Average-data**: Industry averages per unit purchased
- **Spend-based**: Emissions per currency spent

**Key Data Sources:**
- Supplier emissions reports
- Lifecycle assessment databases (ecoinvent, DEFRA)
- Economic input-output (EIO) factors

#### Category 2: Capital Goods
Upstream emissions from production of capital goods purchased by the organization.

**Includes:**
- Equipment and machinery
- Buildings and facilities
- Vehicles
- IT infrastructure

**Approach:** Amortize over expected lifetime or report in year of purchase.

#### Category 3: Fuel and Energy-Related Activities (FERA)
Emissions not included in Scope 1 or 2:
- Upstream emissions of purchased fuels (extraction, production, transport)
- Upstream emissions of purchased electricity
- Transmission and distribution (T&D) losses
- Generation of purchased electricity sold to end users

#### Category 4: Upstream Transportation and Distribution
Transportation and distribution of products purchased by the organization:
- Inbound logistics
- Logistics between facilities
- Third-party logistics providers

**Calculation:**
```
Emissions = Distance × Weight × Transport Emission Factor
```

#### Category 5: Waste Generated in Operations
Disposal and treatment of waste from the organization's operations:
- Landfill
- Recycling
- Incineration
- Composting
- Wastewater treatment

**Waste Emission Factors (Examples):**
| Waste Type | Disposal Method | kg CO2e/tonne |
|------------|----------------|---------------|
| Mixed waste | Landfill | 579 |
| Paper/card | Recycling | 21 |
| Plastics | Incineration | 2,280 |
| Food waste | Composting | 10 |
| Electronics | Recycling | 2,100 |

#### Category 6: Business Travel
Transportation of employees for business purposes:
- Air travel
- Rail travel
- Road travel (rental cars, taxis)
- Hotel stays (optional)

**Air Travel Factors (Example):**
| Class | kg CO2e/passenger-km |
|-------|---------------------|
| Economy | 0.148 |
| Premium Economy | 0.237 |
| Business | 0.429 |
| First Class | 0.591 |

#### Category 7: Employee Commuting
Transportation of employees between homes and worksites:
- Private vehicles
- Public transport
- Walking/cycling
- Remote work reduction

**Data Collection Methods:**
- Employee surveys
- Average commute data by region
- Transport mode assumptions

#### Category 8: Upstream Leased Assets
Emissions from assets leased by the organization (not included in Scope 1/2):
- Leased buildings
- Leased vehicles
- Leased equipment

*Note: Reporting depends on consolidation approach used.*

### Downstream Categories (9-15)

#### Category 9: Downstream Transportation and Distribution
Transportation and distribution of sold products:
- Outbound logistics
- Distribution to retail
- Customer delivery

*Only if not paid for by reporting company.*

#### Category 10: Processing of Sold Products
Processing of intermediate products by downstream companies.

**Relevant for:**
- Raw material producers
- Component manufacturers
- Industrial chemical suppliers

#### Category 11: Use of Sold Products
Emissions from use of goods and services sold by the organization:
- Direct use-phase emissions (fuel, electricity)
- Indirect use-phase emissions (consumables)

**Significant for:**
- Vehicle manufacturers
- Appliance makers
- Electronics companies
- Fuel producers

#### Category 12: End-of-Life Treatment of Sold Products
Disposal of products sold by the organization:
- Consumer disposal methods
- Product recyclability
- Material recovery

#### Category 13: Downstream Leased Assets
Emissions from assets owned by organization and leased to others:
- Leased buildings
- Leased equipment
- Leased vehicles

#### Category 14: Franchises
Emissions from franchise operations:
- Scope 1 and 2 of franchisees
- Not applicable if not a franchisor

#### Category 15: Investments
Emissions from investments and financing:
- Equity investments
- Debt investments
- Project finance
- Managed investments

**Relevant for:**
- Financial institutions
- Investment companies
- Banks
- Asset managers

## Prioritizing Scope 3 Categories

Not all categories are relevant to every organization. Prioritize based on:

### Size
Categories representing the largest portion of emissions.

### Influence
Categories where the organization can drive change.

### Risk
Categories with regulatory, reputational, or financial risk.

### Stakeholder Interest
Categories important to investors, customers, and regulators.

### Data Availability
Categories where quality data can be obtained.

## Data Quality Hierarchy

1. **Primary data** from suppliers (most accurate)
2. **Secondary data** from LCA databases
3. **Spend-based estimates** using EIO factors
4. **Proxy data** from similar products/activities

## Reduction Strategies by Category

### Category 1 (Purchased Goods)
- Supplier engagement programs
- Low-carbon procurement criteria
- Circular economy initiatives

### Category 4 (Transportation)
- Optimize logistics networks
- Modal shift to rail/sea
- Local sourcing

### Category 6 (Business Travel)
- Virtual meeting policies
- Train vs. plane policies
- Carbon offsets

### Category 11 (Product Use)
- Energy-efficient product design
- Renewable energy integration
- Product-as-a-service models

## Best Practices

1. **Start with screening** - Estimate all categories first
2. **Focus resources** on material categories
3. **Engage suppliers** for better primary data
4. **Set reduction targets** aligned with science
5. **Track annually** with consistent methodology
6. **Disclose transparently** including methodology and limitations
