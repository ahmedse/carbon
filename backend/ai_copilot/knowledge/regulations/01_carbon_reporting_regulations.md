# Carbon Reporting Regulations Overview

## Introduction

This document provides an overview of major carbon reporting regulations and frameworks worldwide. Organizations must understand which regulations apply to their operations and ensure compliance.

## European Union Regulations

### EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)

**Effective:** January 2024 (phased implementation)

**Scope:**
- Large EU companies (2 of 3: >250 employees, >€40M revenue, >€20M assets)
- Listed SMEs (from 2026)
- Non-EU companies with >€150M EU revenue (from 2028)

**Requirements:**
- Report according to European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)
- Double materiality assessment (impact and financial)
- Scope 1, 2, and material Scope 3 emissions
- Science-based targets if set
- Third-party assurance (limited initially, reasonable by 2028)

**ESRS Climate Standards:**
- E1: Climate change
- Transition plan disclosure
- GHG reduction targets
- Carbon credits and offsets

### EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)

**Coverage:**
- Power generation
- Manufacturing industries
- Commercial aviation (intra-EU)
- Maritime shipping (from 2024)

**Requirements:**
- Monitor and report annual CO2 emissions
- Surrender allowances equal to emissions
- Third-party verification
- Phase 4 (2021-2030): Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)

### Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)

**Effective:** Transitional period 2023-2025, full operation from 2026

**Covered Products:**
- Iron and steel
- Cement
- Aluminum
- Fertilizers
- Electricity
- Hydrogen

**Requirements:**
- EU importers must purchase CBAM certificates
- Price linked to EU ETS allowance price
- Third-country producers must provide emissions data
- Phased introduction replacing free allocations

## United Kingdom Regulations

### Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)

**Scope:**
- Large unquoted companies
- Large LLPs
- Quoted companies

**Thresholds (2 of 3):**
- >250 employees
- >£36M turnover
- >£18M balance sheet

**Requirements:**
- UK energy consumption (kWh)
- Associated Scope 1 and 2 emissions
- Energy intensity ratio
- Energy efficiency actions
- Included in annual Directors' Report

### Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) - UK

**Mandatory for:**
- Premium listed companies (from 2021)
- Standard listed companies (from 2022)
- Large private companies (from 2023)
- Asset managers and owners

**Four Pillars:**
1. Governance
2. Strategy
3. Risk Management
4. Metrics and Targets

### UK Emissions Trading Scheme (UK ETS)

**Replaced EU ETS post-Brexit**

**Coverage:**
- Power generation
- Energy-intensive industries
- Aviation (domestic and UK-EEA)

**Threshold:** >20MW thermal input

## United States Regulations

### SEC Climate Disclosure Rule (2024)

**Scope:** All SEC registrants (public companies)

**Requirements:**
- Scope 1 and 2 emissions (phased by filer size)
- Scope 3 if material or target-included (deferred)
- Climate-related risks and opportunities
- Governance and risk management
- GHG reduction targets and transition plans
- Third-party assurance (phased)

**Timeline:**
- Large accelerated filers: FY 2025
- Accelerated filers: FY 2026
- Smaller reporting companies: FY 2027

### California Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (SB 253)

**Scope:** Companies doing business in California with >$1B revenue

**Requirements:**
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions disclosure
- Third-party verification
- Public disclosure through CARB

**Timeline:**
- Scope 1 & 2: 2026 (for 2025 data)
- Scope 3: 2027 (for 2026 data)

### California Climate-Related Financial Risk Act (SB 261)

**Scope:** Companies doing business in California with >$500M revenue

**Requirements:**
- TCFD-aligned climate risk disclosure
- Biennial reporting

### EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program (GHGRP)

**Scope:** Facilities emitting >25,000 tonnes CO2e/year

**Requirements:**
- Annual GHG emissions reporting
- Source-specific calculation methods
- Third-party verification (certain sectors)
- Public disclosure through FLIGHT database

## International Frameworks

### ISSB (IFRS S1 and S2)

**International Sustainability Standards Board**

**IFRS S1: General Requirements**
- Sustainability-related risks and opportunities
- Governance, strategy, risk management
- Metrics and targets

**IFRS S2: Climate-Related Disclosures**
- Climate-related risks and opportunities
- Transition and physical risks
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions
- Industry-specific metrics

**Adoption:** Voluntary globally; mandatory in some jurisdictions

### CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project)

**Voluntary but widely expected:**
- Climate questionnaire
- Supply chain program
- City-level reporting
- Scoring (A to D-)

**Who requests:**
- Investors ($130+ trillion AUM)
- Customers (280+ members)
- Cities and governments

### Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)

**Framework for target-setting:**
- 1.5°C aligned targets
- Near-term (5-10 year) targets
- Net-zero targets
- Validation and monitoring

**Sectors with specific guidance:**
- Financial institutions
- Oil and gas
- Buildings
- Transport
- Apparel and footwear

### GRI Standards

**Global Reporting Initiative:**
- GRI 305: Emissions
- Scope 1, 2, 3 reporting
- Intensity metrics
- Reduction initiatives

## Regional Regulations

### Asia-Pacific

**Japan:**
- Climate-related disclosure for prime market listed companies
- TCFD-aligned

**Singapore:**
- SGX listed companies: TCFD disclosure
- Climate reporting requirements expanding

**Australia:**
- NGER (National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting)
- Mandatory for >25kt CO2e or energy threshold
- Climate disclosure for large entities (from 2024)

**China:**
- Mandatory disclosure for heavy emitters
- National ETS (power sector)
- Expanding to other sectors

### Canada

**Federal:**
- GHGRP for facilities >10kt CO2e
- TCFD-aligned disclosure for federally regulated financial institutions

**Proposed:**
- Mandatory climate disclosure for large companies

## Best Practices for Compliance

1. **Identify applicable regulations** based on jurisdiction, size, and sector
2. **Map requirements** to existing data and systems
3. **Implement robust data collection** for all scopes
4. **Establish governance** for climate reporting
5. **Engage third-party assurance** providers early
6. **Monitor regulatory developments** as frameworks evolve
7. **Align with multiple frameworks** where possible (CDP, TCFD, GRI)
