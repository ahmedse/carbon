# Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)

## Overview

The Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is a partnership between CDP, the United Nations Global Compact, World Resources Institute (WRI), and the World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF). It enables companies to set emissions reduction targets in line with climate science.

## What Are Science-Based Targets?

Science-based targets are GHG emissions reduction goals that align with the level of decarbonization required to meet the Paris Agreement goal of limiting global warming to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.

## Target Types

### Near-Term Targets

**Timeframe:** 5-10 years from submission

**Requirements:**
- Cover minimum 95% of Scope 1 and 2 emissions
- Cover minimum 67% of Scope 3 emissions (if material)
- 1.5°C aligned for Scope 1 and 2
- Well-below 2°C aligned for Scope 3 (minimum)

**Reduction Rate (1.5°C):**
- 4.2% absolute reduction per year
- 42% reduction over 10 years from base year

### Net-Zero Targets

**Timeframe:** No later than 2050

**Requirements:**
- Near-term target as prerequisite
- Long-term science-based target
- 90% absolute reduction in Scope 1, 2, and 3
- Neutralize residual emissions (≤10%) with permanent carbon removal

### FLAG Targets (Forest, Land and Agriculture)

**Required for:** Companies with >20% emissions from FLAG activities

**Sectors:**
- Food production
- Agriculture
- Forest products
- Paper and pulp
- Tobacco

**Requirements:**
- Separate FLAG and non-FLAG targets
- Include land-related emissions and removals
- No-deforestation commitment

## Scope 3 Requirements

### When is Scope 3 Mandatory?

Scope 3 target required when Scope 3 represents more than 40% of total emissions.

### Screening Threshold

Companies must:
1. Screen all 15 Scope 3 categories
2. Include categories representing cumulatively 67% of Scope 3
3. May exclude categories with limited data if justified

### Scope 3 Target Options

**Absolute Target:**
```
Reduce Scope 3 emissions X% by YEAR from BASE YEAR
```

**Supplier Engagement Target:**
```
X% of suppliers (by emissions) will set science-based targets by YEAR
```

**Customer Engagement Target:**
```
X% of customers (by emissions) will set science-based targets by YEAR
```

## Target Validation Process

### Step 1: Commit
- Submit commitment letter
- Added to SBTi website as "committed"
- 24-month window to submit targets

### Step 2: Develop Targets
- Calculate base year emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3)
- Use SBTi target-setting tools
- Choose target type and ambition

### Step 3: Submit for Validation
- Complete target submission form
- Provide supporting documentation
- Pay validation fee

### Step 4: Validation
- SBTi team reviews submission
- Clarification questions may be sent
- ~30 business days timeline

### Step 5: Announcement
- Validated targets announced on SBTi website
- Use of SBTi logo permitted
- Annual progress disclosure required

## Target Setting Methods

### Absolute Contraction Approach
```
Target = (1 - Reduction Rate)^Years × Base Year Emissions
```

**Example:**
- Base year emissions: 100,000 tCO2e
- Reduction rate: 4.2% per year
- Target year: 10 years
- Target: 100,000 × (1 - 0.042)^10 = 65,021 tCO2e

### Sectoral Decarbonization Approach (SDA)

Used for sectors with specific intensity pathways:
- Power generation
- Iron and steel
- Cement
- Aluminum
- Buildings

### Economic Intensity Approach

For service companies:
```
Target = Reduce emissions per unit revenue by X%
```

## Validation Criteria

### 1. Boundary
- Scope 1 and 2 must cover minimum 95% of emissions
- Consolidated according to GHG Protocol

### 2. Timeframe
- Target year 5-10 years from submission
- Base year no more than 2 years before submission

### 3. Ambition
- Scope 1 & 2: 1.5°C aligned (4.2% per year)
- Scope 3: Well-below 2°C minimum (2.5% per year)

### 4. Scope 3 Threshold
- Required if >40% of total emissions
- Must cover 67% of Scope 3 emissions

### 5. Target Structure
- Clear quantitative commitment
- Specified base year and target year
- Specified scope coverage

## Tracking and Reporting

### Annual Requirements
- Report progress through CDP or public disclosure
- Update inventory if significant changes
- Maintain GHG Protocol compliant methodology

### Recalculation Triggers
- Structural changes (M&A, divestments)
- Methodology changes
- Errors discovered in base year

### Target Revalidation
- Required every 5 years
- Or when targets no longer aligned with criteria
- When significant recalculation needed

## Sector-Specific Guidance

### Financial Institutions
- Separate guidance for financed emissions
- Portfolio-level targets
- Sectoral decarbonization pathways

### Oil and Gas
- Scope 3 Category 11 (use of sold products) required
- Near-term absolute reduction targets
- Phase-down of fossil fuel production

### Buildings
- Intensity targets (kgCO2e/m²)
- Sectoral decarbonization approach
- Separate residential and commercial

### Transport
- Vehicle manufacturers
- Fleet operators
- Aviation and shipping

## Common Challenges

### Data Quality
- Scope 3 data often estimated
- Supplier data gaps
- Calculation methodology inconsistencies

### Scope 3 Coverage
- Meeting 67% threshold
- Influencing value chain emissions
- Tracking supplier progress

### Business Changes
- M&A impacts on targets
- Organic growth considerations
- Divesting high-emission assets

## Best Practices

1. **Start with robust inventory** - Quality base year is essential
2. **Engage leadership early** - Board-level commitment required
3. **Map reduction levers** - Identify decarbonization pathways before target-setting
4. **Engage value chain** - Supplier engagement critical for Scope 3
5. **Integrate with strategy** - Align targets with business planning
6. **Communicate transparently** - Disclose methodology and progress
7. **Plan for updates** - Build processes for annual tracking
