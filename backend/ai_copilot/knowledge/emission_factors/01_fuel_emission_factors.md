## Data Sources

These emission factors are derived from:
- UK Government GHG Conversion Factors (DEFRA/BEIS)
- US EPA Emission Factors Hub
- IPCC Guidelines for National GHG Inventories
- IEA Emissions Factors Database

**Note:** Emission factors are updated annually. Always use the most recent factors available for your reporting year.

## Usage Notes

1. **Gross vs Net Calorific Value**: Know which your data represents
2. **Biofuel content**: Standard fuels may contain biofuel (adjust factors)
3. **Regional variations**: Fuel composition varies by region
4. **WTT factors**: Include for complete Scope 3 Category 3 reporting
5. **Updates**: Check for annual updates to factors
