# AAST Carbon Platform
## Executive Summary

---

### What It Does

A carbon management system for AAST. Tracks emissions, calculates footprint, generates reports—all in one place instead of spreadsheets.

---

### Core Features

**Emissions Tracking**
- All three scopes: direct (fuel, vehicles), electricity, and value chain (transport, procurement)
- Organized by campus, department, or activity
- Full historical records

**Automated Calculations**
- International emission factors applied automatically
- GHG Protocol-compliant methodology
- Audit-ready documentation

**Dashboards**
- Executive summary for leadership
- Detailed breakdowns for operations
- Target tracking against reduction goals

**AI Assistant**
Built-in AI that understands carbon data:
- Ask in plain language: *"What's our biggest emission source?"* or *"Compare this quarter to last year"*
- Get explanations, not just numbers
- Identifies anomalies and trends automatically
- Suggests where to focus reduction efforts
- Answers questions instantly—no need to wait for analyst reports
- Learns AAST's specific context over time

Makes carbon expertise accessible to anyone, not just specialists.

---

### Why It Matters

1. **Compliance** — Ready for upcoming Egyptian environmental regulations
2. **Cost insight** — Emissions map to energy spend; find waste, save money
3. **Credibility** — Real data for accreditation bodies and partners
4. **Revenue potential** — Foundation for VVB accreditation and carbon consulting services

---

### Recommendation

Expand adoption across departments. The platform is ready—value grows with usage.
